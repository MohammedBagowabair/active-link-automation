## Platform

## Stack

delegated: Vite, React, and TypeScript. Static only. Production base path `/active-link-automation/` for GitHub Pages. No backend, no database, no GitHub Actions workflow. The brief allowed Vite + React or vanilla and left the choice open.

## Users

Property owners and site managers in Nilai, Seremban, Negeri Sembilan, Selangor, and surrounding areas. Confirmed audiences: residential, commercial, industrial, and agricultural and remote. They are deciding who to call for CCTV, alarms, access, attendance, autogate, barrier gates, automation, or electrical work, often after a fault (offline camera, power trip, gate) or before a new install.

## Product Principles

- Say only what was verified. A missing fact stays missing.
- The first screen offers a call, a WhatsApp chat, or a quote. Proof sits next to that offer.
- The visitor assembles the quote. Home, shop, factory, and farm change which systems start ticked. The visitor can edit every tick.
- Repair language names the symptom and the fact that the team works on it. It does not invent a cause.
- The page works on a phone in one hand, including with motion reduced.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG contrast on text. Visible keyboard focus. The dock, symptom keys, site switch, and quote form are usable by touch and keyboard. `prefers-reduced-motion` stops the reticle sweep and other non-essential motion. The hero graphic stays flat.
