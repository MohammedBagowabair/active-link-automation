# Surface: Active Link marketing site

Mode: Persuade.

Audience: owners and managers of homes, shops, factories, and farms around Nilai who need a security, automation, or electrical team. Job: decide and make contact. Action: call, WhatsApp, or send a quote. Proof: Google 5.0 from 1,070+ reviews, three verbatim quotes, named partner badges, published hours and address. Constraints: static only, verified facts only, production base `/active-link-automation/`, mobile dock, reduced motion, no cream sections.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a single night-shift viewfinder for a Nilai workshop that installs and repairs security and power. It refuses the split hero and the equal icon-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Void `#04060c`, working light `#00D4FF`, violet bloom `#8B6CFF` only behind the lens and the system builder, brand blue `#0058A8` on the wordmark, brand red `#A21827` only as a filled mark with light text. Plus Jakarta Sans. Controls are HUD keys. Corner brackets frame the viewfinder. Glass plates appear only on the builder, reviews, and contact. Grid and scan lines stay inside the viewfinder.

STORY: The visitor sees one team for CCTV, alarms, access, attendance, gates, automation, and electrical, trusts the rating, and calls, messages, or builds a quote that names the site and the systems.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Edge-to-edge viewfinder. The top bracket is the wordmark and navigation. The H1 is Security. Automation. Electrical. at display scale, with One team. Complete solutions. as the lede directly under it. A CSS 3D camera sits in the right field at a resting tilt; its glow may cross the gutter, the glyphs stay clear. A full-width control strip at the bottom of the viewfinder holds Call, WhatsApp, and Build a quote. Coordinates sit in the lower bracket as data. The stats rail is the next section, not more hero.

SIGNATURE: Build your system. Home, Shop, Factory, and Farm rewrite a line schematic and the ticked systems. The quote form is that same state. CCTV symptom keys are the second interaction. Motion is the lens sweep plus that reconfiguration. Reduced motion keeps both usable and still.

FORM: Brief-pinned futuristic HUD, the user's stated world, not a rolled candidate. Seed key: brief-pinned.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
